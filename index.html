DEFENSE-GRADE STANDARDS

Production Architecture & Scalability

Designed for instant institutional adoption across military academies and NDA training wings with zero hardware procurement barriers:

⚡

Frontend & PWA Engine

React 18, TypeScript, Three.js 3D terrain canvas, and Offline PWA Service Worker caching for air-gapped field laptops.

🚀

Backend & Mutation

FastAPI 0.111 on Python 3.11 with non-blocking WebSockets delivering delta updates (< 5 KB/s) for ultra-low latency.

🏔️

Terrain Physics

SRTM 30m Digital Elevation Models calculating line-of-sight knife-edge diffraction to realistically shadow radio propagation.

🤖

Bi-Temporal AAR Engine

PostgreSQL 15 event store paired with Google Gemini AI for instant tactical scoring, timeline scrubbing, and debrief transcripts.
